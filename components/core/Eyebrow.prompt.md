Rite's signature kicker label: 12px, semibold, uppercase, 0.18em tracking. It opens almost every slide and every page header.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Market intelligence</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="mint">Enterprise AI · 1 of 2</Eyebrow>
```

Use `·` (middle dot) to separate parts, never a hyphen. Pair with a sentence-case headline directly below it. `tone="mint"` on dark surfaces, `brand` on light.
